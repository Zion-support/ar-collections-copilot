AR Collections Copilot

A proposed receivables review process: aging analysis, exceptions and follow-up planning with human approval.

Current implementation status

This is documentation and an informational page, not an implemented collections engine. Banking/ERP integration, production readiness, SSO, RBAC, encryption and automatic messaging have not been established here.

Not legal or credit advice. Do not upload debtor identities, credentials or customer payment data. No external contact, credit decision or payment is authorized.

Define a responsible pilot

  1. Name the receivables owner and confirm applicable communication rules.
  2. Use synthetic invoices, reconcile balances and define aging buckets and exceptions.
  3. Require approval before follow-up. Stop on disputes or inconsistent balances; do not contact debtors automatically.
